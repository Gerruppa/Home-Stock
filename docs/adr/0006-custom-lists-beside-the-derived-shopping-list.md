# Custom Lists beside the derived Shopping List

Shopping for an occasion (a dinner, guests) needs things the stock does not call for: more cream than Minimum Stock asks, or candles that are not stock at all. These go on a **Custom List**, a separate, hand-kept list with a name and an optional date, and never on the Shopping List itself.

The Shopping List stays derived: every line says why it is there ("poniżej minimum", "wystarczy na ok. N dni"), and buying it takes the Product off. A line written by hand has no such reason and would not leave when bought, and mixing them would lose what was bought for the occasion. Keeping the two apart also lets several occasions be prepared at once and deleted when they are over.

A List Line is a Product or free text: a Product line can become a Purchase, while free text covers what is bought but not kept as stock. A Product on both the Shopping List and a Custom List shows on each with its own Quantity; nothing is merged.
